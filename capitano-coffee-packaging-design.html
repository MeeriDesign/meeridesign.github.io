Capitano Coffee Packaging Design

This coffee packaging project was my BA thesis, created to make Capitano stand out with bold illustrations and a unique color palette. The brand is inspired by a fictional captain, sailing in search of the world’s finest coffee.

Each illustration tells part of his story and highlights a different brewing method. The visual style draws from 9th–10th century Nishapur ceramics, giving the brand a sense of cultural richness and authenticity. Bright colors and a clean white background make it eye-catching on the shelf.
