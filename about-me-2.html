About Me

Hi!

I’m Maryam Moradi — or Meeri, as some of y’all may know me — a visual designer and illustrator with a passion for branding, packaging, and storytelling through visuals. I graduated with a BA in Visual Communication and love creating designs that blend cultural depth with contemporary aesthetics.

Whether I’m illustrating a character or designing an identity, I care about details, emotions, and making ideas shine. Let’s create something meaningful together.
