Reverse Universe Posters

This project was inspired by a childhood thought experiment: What if the roles of humans and animals were reversed, and animals held the dominant power?

The result is a series of unsettling posters, some viewers have described them as disturbing or even horrifying. But perhaps that’s the point: to mirror the fear, suffering, and exploitation that animals might feel when faced with human dominance. This reversal invites viewers to confront the ethics of our relationship with animals from an unfamiliar, uncomfortable perspective.
