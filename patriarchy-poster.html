Patriarchy Poster

For generations, girls have lived under the shadow of male guardianship and patriarchal systems, often facing systemic oppression and the denial of their most basic rights. In countries like Afghanistan, many women are still denied education and forced into marriage, treated more as possessions than as individuals with autonomy.

This poster symbolizes the imprisonment of women and girls within the suffocating framework of patriarchy. It reflects the harsh reality faced by those whose lives are shaped by outdated traditions, gender inequality, and institutionalized control.
