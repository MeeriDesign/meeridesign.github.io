Dinemate Application Design

The Problem

Most existing restaurant-finder apps suffer from cluttered interfaces, weak search/filter tools, and overly complex reservation processes. Users often abandon bookings due to confusing flows and lack of useful features.

Project Goal

Dinemate was designed to offer a clean, intuitive experience for discovering and reserving restaurants — with a focus on personalization, simplicity, and ease of use.

My Role & Focus

  • Designed improved search and filtering for preferences like budget, cuisine, and location

  • Streamlined the reservation process for clarity and speed

  • Added features tailored to specific user needs, such as family dining or dietary filters

Research & Key Insights

User surveys revealed several recurring needs:

  • Interactive maps and table selection

  • Menu previews before booking

  • Child-friendly options for families

These findings shaped a user-first design approach and guided the app’s core functionality and interface decisions.
